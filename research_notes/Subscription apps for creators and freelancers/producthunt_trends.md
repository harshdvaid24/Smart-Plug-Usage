> **Method note for the report writer:**
> - **No pages were opened.** WebFetch fails in this environment, and the proxy blocks producthunt.com. Every finding below comes from WebSearch result summaries (35 searches), so wording is paraphrase. Spot-check exact numbers before publishing.
> - **Where the numbers come from:**
>   - Upvote counts and daily ranks come mostly from **hunted.space**, a third-party Product Hunt tracker. A few come from aitoolly.com daily digests.
>   - "Day Rank" values come from snippets of Product Hunt pages.
>   - "#1 Product of the Week" claims come from company press releases.
> - **Dates:** each date is the launch date shown on the listing. Product Hunt listings sometimes show inconsistent "launched in" years, and those are flagged where noticed.
> - **"Promoted"** on a Product Hunt leaderboard means a paid placement, not an organic rank.
> - **Scope:** this file covers creator tools first, then consumer apps.
> - **Today is 2026-10-12.** Launches from mid-2026 (June–October) are thinly covered; see the Gaps sections.

## Q1. Which creator-focused products ranked as Product of the Day/Week/Month or won Golden Kitty Awards in 2025–2026?

### Takeaway
No creator tool showed up as a Golden Kitty winner in the last edition (2024 products, voted January 2025). Product Hunt then **discontinued the Golden Kitty Awards in November 2025** and replaced them with quarterly, traction-based **Orbit Awards**. The first Orbit category was AI Dictation, not a creator category.

Instagram, Reels, Shorts and TikTok tools launched in large numbers in 2025–26, but most of the confirmed placements found are mid-table (#5–#15 on the day). The only top placements found are:
- **Vidau:** #1 Product of the Week, September 2025 (per its own press release)
- **SuperX**, an X-creator growth tool: #3 for the month of February 2026
- **AIThumbnail.so:** #9 on the day

### Inferences
- **Launch rank is a poor demand signal for creator tools.** Creator tools on Product Hunt cluster at #5–#15 on the day (Submagic API #11, AIThumbnail.so #9). #1 needs roughly 300–750 upvotes (PH Wrapped 335 on a Saturday in December 2025, VideoGen 735 in 2024).
- **Product Hunt voters are mostly makers and tech workers, not Instagram creators**, so rank mostly reflects the maker's network. Note that 79% of launches are self-hunted.
- **Award categories now follow traction, not launch day.** With Golden Kitty gone and Orbit judging traction and verified reviews by category (starting with AI Dictation), a creator app's route to Product Hunt recognition in 2026 is a body of verified reviews in a defined category.
- **"Agent" is the 2025–26 positioning language for creator tools** (reap, Inrō AI, Postiz), replacing "AI-powered."
- **Meta's free Edits app** (watermark-free exports, built-in analytics) squeezes paid mobile editors aimed at Instagram-first creators.
- **Creator business admin is the least crowded creator segment on Product Hunt.** Deal pipeline, media kits, invoicing and payout tracking draw few launches (July, MediaKits), while clipping, captions, thumbnails, DM automation and link-in-bio each have many.

- Exact upvotes and ranks for OpusClip, reap, Postiz, Inrō AI, Edits, Captions, Thumbmagic, Linkie and July were not in the search summaries; Product Hunt pages could not be opened.
- No full list of Golden Kitty category winners for 2024 products (e.g., AI Video, Social, Marketing) was found. Syllaby's finalist category is not stated.

## Q2. Which consumer mobile apps for individuals trended on Product Hunt in 2025–2026, and which charge a subscription?

### Takeaway
**Voice dictation was the clearest consumer trend** with an official award. Wispr Flow won Orbit's first People's Champ award for AI Dictation (Winter 2025), and Typeless (an AI voice keyboard for iPhone) appeared on the November and December 2025 monthly leaderboards. Health (Welltory, November 2025) and language learning (Fluently, March 2025) also reached monthly leaderboards.

Below the leaderboards, Product Hunt saw floods of near-identical, iOS-first AI apps:
- photo calorie trackers
- AI speaking tutors
- offline LLM chat apps
- AI journals and habit trackers
- Nano Banana photo wrappers

Where pricing showed up, it was mostly subscriptions or credits, with a few apps positioning against subscriptions (Lekh AI one-time $4.99; MyDeviceAI free and open source).

### Inferences
- **The common failure is trust**: hidden paywalls at export, cancellations that don't work, credits that vanish. Usability is rarely the complaint. A new subscription app aimed at price-sensitive Indian creators could position on **"no watermark, no export trap, cancel in one tap, credits never expire,"** backed by UPI AutoPay or Play/App Store subscriptions. That pricing mechanism is an inference; see the economics notes.
- **Reliability regressions after updates** (OpusClip) suggest that incumbents shipping fast AI features break core workflows. A narrow, dependable tool can win users who value "it just works."
- **Meta's free Edits has a gap**: no desktop and less depth. It still sets the price anchor at ₹0 for basic Instagram editing, so paid editing on mobile is a hard sell.

### Gaps
- The review samples are small (CapCut 34–82 per listing; Captions 2), and Product Hunt reviewers skew toward makers and tech workers. The findings are directional, not representative of Instagram creators.
- Not covered: complaints about Descript, Submagic, Linktree, ManyChat, Beacons and Buffer (not searched).
- Product Hunt ratings for Edits alone were not found. The 4.53 (175 ratings) figure belongs to the Instagram app overall. — [PH Instagram reviews](https://www.producthunt.com/products/instagram/reviews)
