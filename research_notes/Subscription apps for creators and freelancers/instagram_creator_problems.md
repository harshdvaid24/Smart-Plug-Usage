**Interim (batch 1):**
- Meta's Instagram Platform has two configurations, both for Instagram *professional* accounts (business or creator). "Instagram API with Facebook Login for Business" needs the IG account linked to a Facebook Page, and the app user must be able to do admin-equivalent tasks on that Page. "Instagram API with Instagram Login" (Meta's overview calls it "Business Login for Instagram") is for accounts with only an Instagram presence; it needs no Page and runs on graph.instagram.com. Personal (non-professional) accounts have no API path — [Meta: Instagram Platform overview](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/overview); [Meta: Instagram API with Facebook Login](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-facebook-login); [Phyllo guide 2026](https://www.getphyllo.com/post/instagram-api-guide)
- Third-party guides say the Facebook Login path adds hashtag search and extra business metadata (business discovery) — [Phyllo](https://www.getphyllo.com/post/instagram-api-guide); [CreatorFlow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/do-you-need-business-account-instagram-tools/)
- Publishing cap: Meta's pages disagree. Meta's content publishing guide says 50 API-published posts per rolling 24 hours, with carousels counting as one, checkable via the `content_publishing_limit` endpoint. Another Meta page says 100 posts per 24-hour moving period and caps containers at 400 per rolling 24 hours — [Meta: Content Publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing); [Meta: Content Publishing (IG Login)](https://developers.secure.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/content-publishing)
- Meta's current publishing guide covers single image, video, story and reel through the same two-step flow (create container, then publish). Older Meta text said Reels and Stories were unsupported; that page is outdated — [Meta: Content Publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/content-publishing)
- Insights: Graph API v22.0 (Jan 2025) introduced `views` for media and user insights. It deprecated `impressions` (media and user), plus `plays`, `clips_replays_count` and `ig_reels_aggregated_all_plays_count`, for all versions from April 21, 2025 — [Meta: Graph API v22.0 changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/changelog/version22.0); [Meta: Media Insights reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/reference/instagram-media/insights/); [Supermetrics](https://docs.supermetrics.com/docs/instagram-insights-field-changes-march-25-2025); [Emplifi](https://docs.emplifi.io/platform/latest/home/instagram-insights-metrics-deprecation-april-2025)
- Messaging: an app has 24 hours to reply after a user's message. The HUMAN_AGENT tag allows a manual reply within 7 days, and misusing tags can restrict the account's ability to send messages — [Meta: Instagram Messaging](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/messenger-platform/instagram); [Meta: Messenger Platform and IG Messaging API policy](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/messenger-platform/policy/policy-overview)
- Comment-to-DM ("private replies"): the message must go out within 7 days of the comment, and only one message can be sent to that commenter — [Blotato developer guide quoting Meta](https://www.blotato.com/blog/instagram-messaging-api)
- Advanced Access (needed to serve IG professional accounts the developer does not own or manage) requires App Review and Business Verification — [Meta: Instagram Platform overview](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/overview)
- Timing: Meta expected tech-provider access verification to take about 5 business days (2022 blog). A developer on Meta's forum reported business verification stuck "In Review" for 10+ days, blocking an Instagram publishing App Review; a reply said 10 days is "not unusual anymore" — [Meta developer blog 2022](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2022/06/02/access-verification-process-for-tech-provider-apps/); [Meta Community Forums](https://communityforums.atmeta.com/discussions/Questions_Discussions/business-verification-in-review-10-days-%E2%80%94-blocking-app-review-submission/1372323)

*Reach and algorithm volatility*
- A late-April-2026 Reddit post (29 points, 13 comments, via a Reddit mirror snapshot) describes a "reach collapse" for some accounts, from 10k+ views to a cap of 50–100 views. The poster blames Meta's "Andromeda" AI ranking system, but that rests on one user's testing and one agency's reports — [Reddit mirror snapshot, 2026-04-28](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1sxhkt5/snapshots/2026-04-28T15%3A19%3A17.61244Z)
- A small-business podcast claims "the average creator is now down 30% to 50% on their reach" after an April 2026 update. It cites no dataset — [Mums With Hustle podcast ep. 534](https://mumswithhustle.buzzsprout.com/66380/episodes/19101396-mwh-534-the-april-2026-instagram-algorithm-update-every-business-owner-needs-to-hear)
- A Sotrender roundup says several benchmark studies show the same reach downturn while Instagram passed 3 billion MAU (Sept 2025). Its framing is that distribution changed, not demand — [Sotrender](https://www.sotrender.com/blog/?p=58842)
- Jan 2026: Mosseri's year-end post on authenticity and AI drew a creator backlash. One creator with ~2M followers accused him of "gaslighting", and others reported follower reach falling to "2%" (anecdotal, PR-style blog) — [HelloPartner](https://hellopartner.com/head-of-instagram-faces-huge-backlash-from-influencers-claiming-the-platform-failed-to-protect-them-against-ai-slop-in-2025/)
- Mosseri says ranking and recommendations increase how many posts people see, and tells creators to judge by likes per post and views per Reel rather than follower reach. Creators replied with analytics screenshots disputing this — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/instagram-why-content-isnt-reaching-followers-439028)
- Instagram's ranking has favoured smaller original creators since April 2024. In 2025 Mosseri said accounts that mostly repost others' content would stop being recommended to non-followers (secondary source) — [TechCrunch, Apr 30 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/30/instagram-is-updating-its-ranking-systems-to-surface-more-content-from-smaller-original-creators); [WebProNews](https://www.webpronews.com/instagrams-aggregator-purge-original-creators-gain-ground-as-repost-empires-crumble/)

*Getting paid and pricing deals*

*Survey data (ranked signals)*
- CreatorIQ (July 2025, n=300, worldwide, via eMarketer): the top barrier to growing a creator business is platform algorithm changes or volatility (18%), just ahead of lack of consistent brand deals (17%) — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/creators--biggest-threat-business-growth-isn-t-burnout)

Leading causes are creative fatigue (40%), demanding workloads (31%) and constant screen time (27%). **Interim (batch 1):**
- Meta's Instagram Platform has two configurations, both for Instagram *professional* accounts (business or creator). "Instagram API with Facebook Login for Business" needs the IG account linked to a Facebook Page, and the app user must be able to do admin-equivalent tasks on that Page. ## 3. What Instagram/Meta platform constraints limit a third-party mobile or mobile + web app?

### Takeaway
(pending — interim findings below)

### Cited Findings
**Interim (batch 1):**
- Meta's Instagram Platform has two configurations, both for Instagram *professional* accounts (business or creator). "Instagram API with Facebook Login for Business" needs the IG account linked to a Facebook Page, and the app user must be able to do admin-equivalent tasks on that Page. "Instagram API with Instagram Login" (Meta's overview calls it "Business Login for Instagram") is for accounts with only an Instagram presence; it needs no Page and runs on graph.instagram.com. Personal (non-professional) accounts have no API path — [Meta: Instagram Platform overview](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/overview); [Meta: Instagram API with Facebook Login](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-facebook-login); [Phyllo guide 2026](https://www.getphyllo.com/post/instagram-api-guide)
- Third-party guides say the Facebook Login path adds hashtag search and extra business metadata (business discovery) — [Phyllo](https://www.getphyllo.com/post/instagram-api-guide); [CreatorFlow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/do-you-need-business-account-instagram-tools/)
- Publishing cap: Meta's pages disagree. Meta's content publishing guide says 50 API-published posts per rolling 24 hours, with carousels counting as one, checkable via the `content_publishing_limit` endpoint. Another Meta page says 100 posts per 24-hour moving period and caps containers at 400 per rolling 24 hours — [Meta: Content Publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing); [Meta: Content Publishing (IG Login)](https://developers.secure.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/content-publishing)
- Meta's current publishing guide covers single image, video, story and reel through the same two-step flow (create container, then publish). Older Meta text said Reels and Stories were unsupported; that page is outdated — [Meta: Content Publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/content-publishing)
- Insights: Graph API v22.0 (Jan 2025) introduced `views` for media and user insights. It deprecated `impressions` (media and user), plus `plays`, `clips_replays_count` and `ig_reels_aggregated_all_plays_count`, for all versions from April 21, 2025 — [Meta: Graph API v22.0 changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/changelog/version22.0); [Meta: Media Insights reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/reference/instagram-media/insights/); [Supermetrics](https://docs.supermetrics.com/docs/instagram-insights-field-changes-march-25-2025); [Emplifi](https://docs.emplifi.io/platform/latest/home/instagram-insights-metrics-deprecation-april-2025)
- Messaging: an app has 24 hours to reply after a user's message. The HUMAN_AGENT tag allows a manual reply within 7 days, and misusing tags can restrict the account's ability to send messages — [Meta: Instagram Messaging](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/messenger-platform/instagram); [Meta: Messenger Platform and IG Messaging API policy](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/messenger-platform/policy/policy-overview)
- Comment-to-DM ("private replies"): the message must go out within 7 days of the comment, and only one message can be sent to that commenter — [Blotato developer guide quoting Meta](https://www.blotato.com/blog/instagram-messaging-api)
- Advanced Access (needed to serve IG professional accounts the developer does not own or manage) requires App Review and Business Verification — [Meta: Instagram Platform overview](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/overview)
- Timing: Meta expected tech-provider access verification to take about 5 business days (2022 blog). A developer on Meta's forum reported business verification stuck "In Review" for 10+ days, blocking an Instagram publishing App Review; a reply said 10 days is "not unusual anymore" — [Meta developer blog 2022](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2022/06/02/access-verification-process-for-tech-provider-apps/); [Meta Community Forums](https://communityforums.atmeta.com/discussions/Questions_Discussions/business-verification-in-review-10-days-%E2%80%94-blocking-app-review-submission/1372323)
**Interim (batch 2):**
- `follower_demographics` (age, gender, city, country) is a lifetime metric that isn't returned for accounts with fewer than 100 followers. `engaged_audience_demographics` isn't returned with fewer than 100 engagements in the timeframe, doesn't support since/until (e.g. `this_month` = last 30 days), and covers only the top 45 values — [Meta: Instagram Account Insights reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/api-reference/instagram-user/insights)
- A third-party integration doc says `follower_count`, `online_followers` and all `audience_*` metrics are unavailable below 100 followers. Demographics are returned only to the authenticated account owner, not for third-party lookups of other accounts — [Phyllo 2026](https://www.getphyllo.com/post/instagram-audience-demographics-for-influencer-marketing-%20platforms); [Boomi changelog](https://help.boomi.com/docs/Atomsphere/Data_Integration/APIUpgradesChangelog/2025/2025-01-instagram-upgrade-v21)
- Dec 2025 (Meta developer blog dated Dec 3, 2025, via coverage): the Insights API added Reels skip rate, repost counts at media and account level, and crossposted (Facebook) view metrics. The skip-rate window is fixed at 3 seconds and can't be configured — [exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/digital-news/instagram-expands-marketing-api-with-new-metrics-and-content-management-tools-149985.html); [Storrito](https://storrito.com/resources/how-instagrams-updated-marketing-api-metrics-work/)
- ManyChat's comment-to-DM flows (core on every tier) run inside the Instagram Messaging rules above: a user comment or DM opens the window, and the bot then sends one private reply (within 7 days) and keeps chatting within 24 hours of each user message — [Blabla](https://blabla.ai/blog/manychat-pricing); [Blotato](https://www.blotato.com/blog/instagram-messaging-api); [Meta policy](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/messenger-platform/policy/policy-overview)
**Interim (batch 6)**
- DM rate limits: vendor summaries of Meta's Messenger Platform rate-limits page (updated March 2026) give 750 calls per hour for private replies to comments on posts and Reels, plus per-thread caps against sending too many messages to one thread. The widely repeated "200 automated DMs per hour" looks like a vendor pacing convention, not a Meta rule; no Meta document backs it — [Inro glossary](https://www.inro.social/glossary/instagram-rate-limit); [Rocket](https://www.rocket.new/blog/instagram-comment-automation-api-safe-guide); [InstantDM (200/hr claim)](https://instantdm.com/guides/instagram-ai-agent-rate-limits-safety)
